title: "Incident Report: Final"
owner: Incident Lead
used: For every P1 and P2 incident, within 10 working days after the incident is resolved; for P3 when asked
summary: The complete record of an incident: what happened, how it was found, what it affected, how the SOC responded, the root cause, and what will change. Written for management, auditors and, if needed, regulators.
