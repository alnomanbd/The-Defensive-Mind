---
id: TPL-11
title: Situation Report (sitrep) for Stakeholders
owner: Incident Lead
used: During every P1 and P2 incident at the agreed update interval (every 1 hour for P1, every 4 hours for P2), and at every change of incident lead
summary: A one-page update during a live incident. Same structure every time, so readers can see in seconds what changed since the last report, what is happening now, and what is needed from them.
