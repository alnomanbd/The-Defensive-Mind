title: Incident Timeline and Decision Log
owner: Incident Lead (kept by a named scribe)
used: Opened when a P1 or P2 incident is declared and kept up to date until closure
summary: The running record of an incident: every important event, action and decision with its time, who did it, and the evidence. It is the source for situation reports, the final report and the post-incident review.

> TIP: Use one time zone for every entry, preferably UTC, and say which one above. Mixed time zones are the most common reason timelines cannot be trusted later.

## 2. How to fill in this log

- One row per event, action or decision. Add rows as they happen, not from memory at the end.
- "Type" is one of: Attacker activity, Detection, Action, Decision, Communication, Severity change, Handover.
- Times of attacker activity come from logs and evidence; record the evidence reference.
- Record decisions with the reason and the alternatives considered. This protects the people who made them.
- Never edit an earlier row. If something was wrong, add a new row that corrects it.
