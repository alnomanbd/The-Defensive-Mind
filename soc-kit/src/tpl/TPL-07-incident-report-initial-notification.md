---
id: TPL-07
title: "Incident Report: Initial Notification"
owner: Incident Lead
used: Within 1 hour of declaring a P1 or P2 incident (4 hours for P3 when stakeholders must be told), following SOP-05 and SOP-43
summary: The first written notice of a security incident. Short, factual, sent fast. It tells leaders what is known, what is not known yet, what the SOC is doing and what it needs from them.
---
